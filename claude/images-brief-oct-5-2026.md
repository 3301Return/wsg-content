## The one process change from October 1

**Do not edit the drafts.** A QA pass is editing them at the same time. Instead, write a precise `anchor` for every manifest entry (the first 60 characters of the exact draft line the image should follow, copied from the file, usually a table's last row, a list's last item, or the paragraph the image illustrates). The coordinator runs `python3 scripts/images/insert_images.py {slug} drafts/draft-{slug}-v1.md` after QA, which inserts the image lines after the anchored block. Check your anchors yourself with `--dry-run` (it reports any anchor it can't find without touching the file). Anchors in the intro (before the first H2) are rejected; pick a body line.

Everything else from the October 1 brief holds: the shared toolkit, the dataviz skill before any chart, every number asserted against the article, original work only, captions in Stephen's voice with no dashes, alt text of 10 to 20 words, PNG at most 2200 px wide, `published/assets/images/{slug}/NN-short-name.png`, `manifest.json` with `file`, `alt`, `caption`, `method`, `anchor`, an idempotent `scripts/images/build_{slug}.py` that rebuilds the workbook, asserts the numbers, renders every image and writes the manifest in one run, workbooks under `scripts/images/models/`, no identifiers, and a contact-sheet look at every image before you report.

The drafting agents saved the article numbers as plain Python in `scripts/images/models/numbers_{slug}.py` (articles 1 to 4). Import or re-run those scripts to get the exact figures; the workbook must reproduce them with live formulas, and `recalc_values()` must match every article table cell for cell. If you find a number in the article that disagrees with the script, do not "fix" the article; report it to the coordinator with the section and the two values.
