# Fact sheet: HireVue for Finance (recorded one-way video interviews)

Compiled October 11, 2026 for the WSG article "HireVue for Finance: How to Prepare for a Recorded Interview." Each bullet carries its source URL and the month/year checked. UNVERIFIED marks anything not confirmed against a primary or credible source. The search budget ran out during item 3, so Big 4, BCG and Bain entries rely on direct fetches only.

## 1. HireVue the company

- Founded 2004 by Mark Newman, then a 20-year-old Westminster College student, in South Jordan, Utah; headquartered in Sandy, Utah (per Fortune, Dec 2019). (Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HireVue, Oct 2026) HireVue's About page confirms 2004 and 35+ languages but states no HQ. (Source: https://www.hirevue.com/about, Oct 2026)
- Interview volume, own claims: "70M+" in the About page stats block and "surpassed 80M global interviews" in the history blurb on the same page, unreconciled. (Source: https://www.hirevue.com/about, Oct 2026) Earlier: 24 million+ interviews and 700+ customers by October 2021; 1 million+ interviews in September 2021 alone. (Source: https://www.hirevue.com/press-release/hirevue-customers-conduct-over-1-million-video-interviews-in-just-30-days, Oct 2026)
- Ownership: Carlyle closed a majority investment on November 4, 2019 via Carlyle Partners VII; Granite Ventures, Sequoia, TCV and management kept minority stakes. (Source: https://cooley.com/news/coverage/2019/2019-11-04-the-carlyle-group-closes-majority-investment-in-hirevue, Oct 2026; HireVue's own announcement, Sept 3, 2019: https://www.hirevue.com/blog/hirevue-and-the-carlyle-group-partnering-for-a-new-phase-of-growth) UNVERIFIED: any later ownership change.
- CEOs: Kevin Parker (by 2017), Anthony Reynolds (2022), Jeremy Friedman (Jan 2024). (Source: Wikipedia, Oct 2026)
- Modern Hire acquired May 10, 2023, terms undisclosed. Modern Hire sold role-based "Virtual Job Tryout" assessments to FedEx, Humana and Delta; combined company claimed 1,150+ customers and over half the Fortune 100. (Source: https://huntscanlon.com/hirevue-acquires-modern-hire/, Oct 2026) HireVue then had about 700 clients and 400 staff, Modern Hire about 450 clients and 200 staff (TechTarget, May 2023, via Wikipedia). UNVERIFIED: that Modern Hire came from the 2020 Montage and Shaker merger.

## 2. The format (HireVue's own help center and candidate pages)

- Definition: "an interview you complete through HireVue at your convenience." Most questions need a video answer; some are written or multiple choice. "The company you applied to sets up and manages the interview"; "you won't see the questions until you start." (Source: https://hirevuesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028139552-What-is-an-On-Demand-Interview, Oct 2026)
- What you see first: a requirements page, then pages showing the company, role, "the number and types of questions, and the estimated length," Terms, an optional intro video, an equipment check, then "Try a practice question" or "Continue without practicing." A screen "shows how many attempts you get per question, as set by the hiring company." (Source: https://hirevuesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028433751-Getting-Started-with-Your-On-Demand-Interview-Computer, Oct 2026)
- Each question: prep-time countdown; "Start Recording" skips remaining prep; 3-second countdown; response-time countdown; "Done Answering" ends early, otherwise the answer submits when time runs out. (Source: same article, Oct 2026)
- Time ranges: the help center does not publish configurable ranges (UNVERIFIED). HireVue's 2014 guide gave 30 seconds prep and up to 3 minutes per answer. (Source: https://www.hirevue.com/blog/how-to-take-a-hirevue-interview, Oct 2026) The FAQ: "several minutes to respond to each question," five to eight questions, under 20 to 30 minutes, "set aside 45 minutes to stay on the safe side." (Source: https://www.hirevue.com/candidates/faq, Oct 2026)
- Re-recording: attempts per question are employer-set. Once submitted, "you cannot return to that question." A full retake "requires the company's explicit approval"; HireVue "cannot authorize retakes." (Source: https://hirevuesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028139712-Restart-Your-Interview, Oct 2026)
- Practice: three generic practice questions, "There's no limit" on repeats, "Practice questions are never uploaded or submitted to anyone," and you can replay practice answers but not real ones. (Source: https://hirevuesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360044807611-Practice-your-interview-FAQ, Oct 2026)
- Pausing: no pause control is described. You can close the browser before "Start Now" and return later; after a dropped connection the link "resumes you at the next unanswered question." (Source: https://hirevuesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028139732-Preparing-for-your-Interview, Oct 2026)
- Deadline: "the company that invited you sets the deadline," stated in the invitation (a date/time or a window such as "within 48 hours"); the link expires afterward; HireVue "cannot extend deadlines or reset interviews." (Source: https://hirevuesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028139832-When-is-My-Interview-Due, Oct 2026)
- Devices: Windows 10+ or macOS 10.12+; Chrome (recommended), Firefox, Edge, Safari 15+; "HireVue for Candidates" app on iOS 13+ and Android 8.0+; "Chromium devices are not supported." (Source: https://hirevuesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028139752-Devices-You-Can-Use-to-Take-Your-Interview, Oct 2026) Spec sheet: 350 kbps recommended, 480p recording. (Source: https://legal.hirevue.com/product-documentation/hv-4-technical-specifications, Oct 2026)
- Accommodations: a first-page link lets you request "additional time on timed questions"; anything else goes through the employer. (Source: https://hirevuesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028139512-ADA-Compliance-Steps, Oct 2026) Candidates "can ask for interview questions in advance." (Source: https://www.hirevue.com/candidates/faq, Oct 2026)

## 4. Goldman, JPMorgan, Morgan Stanley specifics

Goldman Sachs
- HireVue replaced first-round summer and full-time analyst interviews from July 2016; second rounds stayed in person. (Source: HR Dive link in table, Oct 2026)
- Goldman's own post (Jan 28, 2020): a "pre-recorded video interview" for "selected candidates," "reviewed by trained human recruiters and colleagues across our firm." Schools interviewed rose from 643 (2015) to 1,250; about one in three summer analyst applicants got a video interview in 2019, under one in five in 2015. (Source: Goldman link in table, Oct 2026)
- eFinancialCareers (Jul 2018): five questions (six if multiple divisions), 30 seconds prep, two-minute answers, one attempt ("one-and-done"), unlimited practice, under 15 minutes total, mostly behavioral plus one division-specific question; recruiters said no review "by robots or anything like that." (Source: https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/2018/07/video-interview-goldman-sachs, Oct 2026)
- IGotAnOffer (Sep 2026): 3 to 6 questions, 30s prep, 1:30 to 2:00 answers, 1 to 3 retries, deadline about one week, after resume screen and before superday; lists DCF and three-statement questions. (Source: https://igotanoffer.com/en/advice/goldman-sachs-hirevue-interview, Oct 2026)
- Glassdoor IB SA (Aug to Sep 2026): six all-behavioral questions (London); invite about a week after applying. (Source: https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/Goldman-Sachs-Investment-Banking-Summer-Analyst-Interview-Questions-EI_IE2800.0,13_KO14,47.htm, Oct 2026)

JPMorgan
- "J.P. Morgan allows three attempts per question" (2018). (Source: eFC link above, Oct 2026)
- IGotAnOffer (Sep 2026): Summer Analyst 3 to 5 questions, 30 to 120s prep, 1.5 to 2 min answers, one optional retry. (Source: https://igotanoffer.com/blogs/finance/jpm-hirevue-interview, Oct 2026)
- Aced (2026): about 3 to 4 questions, 7 days to complete, invite often within hours, superday invites about 2 weeks later. (Source: https://www.aced.io/guides/jpmorgan-investment-banking-summer-analyst-interview, Oct 2026)
- Glassdoor IB SA (Sep 2026): three questions, mostly behavioral; invite 2 to 3 hours after applying; "Tell us about a recent piece of news that you read about that affects the business division you are applying to." (Source: https://www.glassdoor.com/Interview/J-P-Morgan-Investment-Banking-Summer-Analyst-Interview-Questions-EI_IE145.0,10_KO11,44.htm, Oct 2026)

Morgan Stanley (3P only): 3 to 5 questions, 30s prep (Aced) or 60s (IGotAnOffer), 90s to 2 min answers, one retry that deletes the first take, 48 to 72 hour window. (Sources: Aced link in table; https://igotanoffer.com/en/advice/morgan-stanley-hirevue-interview, Oct 2026)

Bank of America and Citi: see table (Aced, Oct 2026).

## 5. AI scoring, regulation and litigation

- Facial analysis: dropped in March 2020, announced January 2021; "visual analysis no longer significantly added value to the assessments." CEO Kevin Parker: "We're primarily assessing the candidate's answer to the questions." Pauses and tonality stayed small factors under bias review. (Source: https://www.shrm.org/mena/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/hirevue-discontinues-facial-analysis-screening, Oct 2026) Fortune (Jan 19, 2021): nonverbal data added about 0.25% of predictive power; the ORCAA audit flagged accents and very short answers as areas to check. (Source: https://fortune.com/2021/01/19/hirevue-drops-facial-monitoring-amid-a-i-algorithm-audit/, Oct 2026)
- EPIC complaint to the FTC, November 6, 2019: alleged deception (denying "facial recognition" while analyzing "facial expressions"), unfairness (secret "employability" scores) and failure to meet OECD AI Principles. No FTC action listed. (Source: https://epic.org/documents/in-re-hirevue/, Oct 2026)
- Illinois Artificial Intelligence Video Interview Act (HB 2557), effective January 1, 2020: notify applicants, explain how the AI works and what it evaluates, obtain consent, share videos only with those whose "expertise or technology is necessary," destroy within 30 days of a request; no penalties stated. (Source: https://shrm.org/topics-tools/employment-law-compliance/illinois-employers-must-comply-artificial-intelligence-video-interview-act, Oct 2026) UNVERIFIED: the 2022 demographic-reporting amendment.
- NYC Local Law 144 of 2021, enforced from July 5, 2023: independent bias audit within one year before use, published summary, at least 10 days' notice (DCWP slides: 10 business days) including the right to request an alternative process; fines up to $500 first violation, $500 to $1,500 after. (Sources: https://www.perkinscoie.com/insights/update/new-york-city-adopts-final-rules-law-governing-automated-employment-decision-tools ; https://www.nyc.gov/site/dca/about/automated-employment-decision-tools.page, Oct 2026)
- ACLU of Colorado complaint, March 19, 2025, to the Colorado Civil Rights Division and EEOC: Intuit's HireVue process allegedly discriminated against a deaf, Indigenous employee denied captioning. HireVue CEO Jeremy Friedman: "entirely without merit" and "Intuit did not use a HireVue AI-based assessment." (Source: https://www.hrdive.com/news/ai-intuit-hirevue-deaf-indigenous-employee-discrimination-aclu/743273/, Oct 2026)
- Illinois BIPA: Deyerler v. HireVue (filed Jan 2022) settled for $3.75 million; 91,305 class members interviewed Jan 27, 2017 to Jun 25, 2026; claims due Oct 13, 2026; final hearing Oct 28, 2026. (Source: https://www.classaction.org/news/3.75m-hirevue-settlement-wraps-up-class-action-lawsuit-over-alleged-biometric-info-violations, Oct 2026)
- JPMorgan "lie detector" case: dismissed as "wholly conclusory" by the district court; First Circuit argument July 28, 2026, no ruling yet. (Source: Courthouse News link in table, Oct 2026) CVS settled a similar Massachusetts suit in July 2024 (HR Dive via Wikipedia, Oct 2026).
- What HireVue scores now: interview responses "are transcribed from speech to text" and scored on language features; games measure cognitive ability, emotional intelligence and personality; low transcription confidence triggers an "Insufficient Data Error" flagged "for manual review." Faces and tone are not listed as inputs. (Source: https://www.hirevue.com/resources/research-paper/hirevues-assessment-science, Oct 2026) FAQ: scoring looks at "the whole context of a response, not just certain words here or there"; HireVue "never uses the video recording of your interview in order to identify you." (Source: https://www.hirevue.com/candidates/faq, Oct 2026) Product page: "AI-scored interviews receive third-party audits, while employers make final decisions." (Source: https://www.hirevue.com/platform/video-interviewing, Oct 2026)
- Software vs humans: AI scoring applies only where an employer buys HireVue Assessments; a plain on-demand interview is watched and rated by people. Goldman says humans review (item 4).

## 6. Recruiter-side facts

- HireVue: reviewers "Review interviews or key moments, apply consistent ratings, and share recordings to keep decisions moving"; Interview Insights adds transcripts, highlights, summaries and talk-time metrics. (Source: https://www.hirevue.com/platform/video-interviewing, Oct 2026)
- Goldman: human recruiters and colleagues review (item 4). Bank of America (3P): most candidates scored by 2 or 3 bankers who then meet; HireVue cuts the shortlist roughly in half. (Source: Aced BofA link, Oct 2026)
- UNVERIFIED: minutes spent per candidate; any bank's public HireVue volume per cycle.

## 7. Question types in finance HireVues

- Motivational: "Why Goldman Sachs, and why investment banking?"; "Why this division?"; (Securities) "Which asset class interests you?" (Source: eFC 2018 link, Oct 2026)
- Behavioral: hardest problem, initiative on a team, conflict with someone senior, greatest strength (eFC 2018); Deloitte: a challenge overcome, a project and results, strengths and weaknesses. (Source: Lawrence link in table, Oct 2026)
- Market or news: JPM Sep 2026 "recent piece of news" prompt (item 4); Citi: news affecting Citi, "pitch me a stock"; BofA: a news item and its market impact. (Sources: Aced Citi and BofA links, Oct 2026)
- Technical: M&I lists occasional three-statement, DCF, WACC and debt-vs-equity questions without naming banks. (Source: https://mergersandinquisitions.com/hirevue-interview/, Oct 2026) IGotAnOffer attributes DCF to Goldman; Aced attributes LBO and terminal value to JPMorgan. UNVERIFIED: a dated candidate report of "walk me through a DCF" at a named bank.
- Situational: HireVue's FAQ lists situational judgment prompts as standard; Wells Fargo: "How would you handle a client request that conflicts with a deadline you've already committed to?" (Sources: HireVue FAQ; Aced Wells Fargo link, Oct 2026)

## 8. Practical prep facts

- Lighting: "Place your light source in front of you"; "lighting from behind makes it hard to see you." (Source: https://www.hirevue.com/candidates/interview-tips, Oct 2026)
- Camera: put the video window "near the top center of the screen." (Source: https://www.hirevue.com/candidates/faq, Oct 2026) Goldman recruiters: camera level with your eyes. (Source: eFC 2018, Oct 2026)
- Eye contact: help center says "Looking at your webcam while speaking gives the feeling of eye contact"; the marketing tips page says "don't worry about making eye contact with the camera. Just be natural!" (Sources: Preparing for your Interview article; interview-tips page, Oct 2026)
- Notes: HireVue: "Keep your notes nearby for reminders during the interview." Goldman recruiters "don't want candidates reading from hidden notes." Bentley: notes allowed but over-reliance "makes you sound robotic." (Sources: interview-tips page; eFC 2018; https://careeredge.bentley.edu/?p=82381, Oct 2026)
- 30-second window: Lawrence advises a quick bullet list during prep, used only if you run dry. M&I: drill 30s prep and 90s answers at 100 to 125 words per minute, about 150 words per answer. (Sources: Lawrence and M&I links, Oct 2026)
- Dress: "wear exactly what you would for a traditional face-to-face interview." (Source: Preparing for your Interview article, Oct 2026) Goldman recruiters said business casual (eFC 2018).
- Environment: "A quiet, distraction free area helps you give the best impression possible." (Source: https://hirevuesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028433631-Tips-for-a-Successful-On-Demand-Interview, Oct 2026)
- Retakes: employer-set; a re-record erases the first take (3P reports); HireVue cannot reset (item 2).

## 9. Timing in the cycle

- Goldman: invite about a week after applying (Glassdoor, Sep 2026); about one week to complete (IGotAnOffer, Sep 2026); response about two weeks later (eFC 2018).
- JPMorgan: invite within hours (Glassdoor: 2 to 3 hours; Aced: often within hours); 7 days to complete (Aced); superday invites about 2 weeks after recording (Aced).
- Others (3P): Citi and BofA about 48 hours; Morgan Stanley 48 to 72 hours; Wells Fargo next day to a week. Deutsche Bank (official): invite about 6 weeks after applying.

## Unverified or conflicting

- Goldman attempts: one (eFC 2018) vs 1 to 3 (IGotAnOffer 2026). Settings change per cycle; say "usually one, check the attempts screen." Question count: five (2018), 3 to 6 (2026 prep site), six (Glassdoor London, Sep 2026).
- Morgan Stanley prep time: 30s vs 60s; whether an AI screen precedes banker review (no primary source).
- No bank has publicly confirmed AI scoring; Goldman says humans review. Prep-site "AI scores nonverbal cues" claims conflict with HireVue dropping facial analysis.
- Barclays: official page lists no video interview; older 3P guides do. UBS and Morgan Stanley: no primary page fetched. BCG Casey, Bain Sova, PwC and KPMG: not confirmed.
- Also unverified: HireVue's configurable time ranges; reviewer minutes per candidate; bank HireVue volumes; current Carlyle stake; Modern Hire's Montage/Shaker origin; Illinois 2022 amendment.
- HireVue's Candidate Insight Report FAQ still says interview questions assess "the emotion you express," stale wording next to the 2021 statements. (Source: https://hirevuesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360028139592-Candidate-Insight-Report-FAQ, Oct 2026)
