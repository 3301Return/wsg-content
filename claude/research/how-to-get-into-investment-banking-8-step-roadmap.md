# Fact sheet: How to Get Into Investment Banking: The 8-Step Undergrad Roadmap

Compiled October 11, 2026 for WallStreetGuide.net. Next open cycle: apply in 2027 (Class of 2029 sophomores, graduating December 2028 to June 2029) for Summer 2028 IB Summer Analyst seats. "Seen" means read in October 2026. Bank-wide numbers cover all internship tracks, not IB alone. The shared web-search budget ran out mid-task; later items rely on direct page fetches, and failed fetches are listed under Unverified.

## 1. Selectivity numbers

- Goldman Sachs 2026 class: about 2,500 interns, acceptance under 1 percent ("fewer than one in 100"), third straight year below 1 percent; Goldman declined to disclose the 2026 application count. (Source: https://www.fortune.com/2026/06/08/exclusive-goldman-sachs-intern-acceptance-rate-falls-below-1-for-third-straight-year/, June 2026)
- Goldman Sachs 2025 class: more than 360,000 applicants, about 2,600 accepted, 0.7 percent; applications up 15 percent on 2024 and 300 percent since 2018; 2024 class hired from more than 475 schools; about one third of the latest partner class began as interns. (Source: https://fortune.com/2025/06/16/goldman-sachs-internship-summer-analysts-wall-street-tpg-olaplex-red-lobster, June 2025)
- Goldman Sachs 2024 class: 315,126 applications, 0.9 percent (Fortune); eFinancialCareers says 315,000 for about 3,000 spots. Goldman plans about 2,500 permanent entry-level hires in July 2026. (Sources: Fortune June 2026; https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/jpmorgan-goldman-sachs-acceptance-rate, July 2024)
- JPMorgan 2025 cycle: about 630,000 applications for roughly 4,100 intern roles, about 0.7 percent (2.8 percent cited for 2023); secondary only, credited to eFinancialCareers. (Source: https://www.entrepreneur.com/business-news/goldman-sachs-accepted-less-than-1-percent-of-intern-applicants-this-year, June 2026)
- JPMorgan earlier disclosure: 493,000 applications for about 4,000 summer analyst and associate seats, cited by Mary Callahan Erdoes at the May 2024 investor day; eFC labels it 2023 in May 2024 (0.91 percent stated; raw math 0.81) and 2024 in July 2024 ("an 82% increase on 2023"). (Sources: https://www.efinancialcareers.co.uk/news/jpmorgan-acceptance-rate-interns-students, May 2024; eFC July 2024 above)
- Bank of America 2026: "nearly 4,000 summer interns and full-time campus recruits" from "more than 500 colleges and universities"; no application count in the release. (Source: https://newsroom.bankofamerica.com/content/newsroom/press-releases/2026/06/bofa-to-welcome-nearly-4000-summer-interns-and-campus-recruits.html, June 3, 2026)
- Bank of America applications, conflicting and secondhand: about 240,000 for fewer than 2,000 internships (about 0.8 percent), per Josh Bronstein, global head of talent, to Business Insider; versus roughly 315,000 for internship and campus programs, attributed to Bloomberg. (Sources: https://goodfest.westfield.com/2026/06/bank-of-america-accepts-less-than-1-of-summer-intern-applications/, June 2026; https://americanbazaaronline.com/2026/06/04/bank-of-america-to-hire-4000-new-recruits-despite-ai-expansion-482174/, June 2026)
- Morgan Stanley and Citi: no US disclosure found. Prospect Rock cites Morgan Stanley EMEA "roughly 57,000 applications for 245 seats," unsourced. (Source: https://prospectrockpartners.com/the-class-of-2029-investment-banking-recruiting-guide-bank-by-bank-timelines-deadlines-and-strategy-for-summer-2028-internships/, July 2026)

## 2. The accelerated timeline

Trackers disagree on exact days but agree that bulge brackets and most elite boutiques opened Summer 2027 IB Summer Analyst applications between November 2025 and February 2026, in applicants' sophomore year. Expect the same months in 2026-2027 for Summer 2028.

- First 2027 SA posting by firm: Houlihan Lokey November 2025; JPMorgan, Citi, Moelis, Lazard, Jefferies, Barclays, Wells Fargo December 2025; Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Bank of America, Evercore, PJT, UBS January 2026; Centerview "open at all times." (Source: https://www.adventiscg.com/class-of-2028-recruiting-timeline, updated September 1, 2026)
- Morgan Stanley US IB 2027: January 1 to February 15, 2026, per the official posting. (Source: https://www.extern.com/post/morgan-stanley-internship-guide, updated October 2026)
- Evercore 2027: January 1 to February 1, 2026 (Superday AI). Evercore 2028 already open: September 9 to November 15, 2026, New York Generalist M&A, graduation December 2028 to June 2029, not rolling. (Sources: https://www.superdayai.com/blog/summer-2028-ib-recruiting-timeline, September 2026; https://www.wallstreetplaybook.org/blog/evercore-2028-summer-analyst-application, September 2026)
- Goldman Sachs conflict: the "classic" Americas IB posting appeared around January 2026 and closed by spring (Superday AI, Adventis); the firm-wide 2027 Summer Analyst application opened August 15, 2026 (Northeastern; Harvard listing August 19 to November 19, 2026). Program is "nine to ten week," usually in the "third or penultimate year." (Sources: https://careers.northeastern.edu/blog/2026/07/29/goldman-sachs-applications-open-august-15/, July 2026; https://careerservices.fas.harvard.edu/jobs/goldman-sachs-2027-summer-analyst-program/, August 2026; https://www.goldmansachs.com/careers/students/programs-and-internships/americas/2027-summer-analyst-program, seen)
- JPMorgan 2027 IB: December 2025 (Adventis, Prospect Rock) vs around March 2026 (Superday AI); closed by July 2026 (Extern). Lazard 2027: December 2025 (Adventis) vs March to April 2026. (Source: https://www.extern.com/post/lazard-internship-guide, updated October 2026)
- Moelis: US Summer Analyst applications "typically open in the December of your sophomore year," 10 weeks. (Source: https://www.moelis.com/careers/explore-opportunities/, seen)
- Centerview: internship "to college juniors"; "Applications for multiple offices will not be reviewed"; asks candidates not to email multiple people at the firm. (Source: https://www.centerview.com/careers, seen)
- Projected openings for Summer 2028 (estimates): Trackr expects Moelis and Houlihan Lokey late November 2026, Barclays early December, Citi, Lazard and Jefferies mid-December, JPMorgan late December, Goldman, Morgan Stanley, UBS, Wells Fargo, Centerview and PJT early January 2027, Bank of America mid-January 2027; Adventis expects all ten in December 2026 or January 2027. Moelis Houston is already open to December 14, 2026 (Extern, October 9). (Sources: https://the-trackr.com/blog/us-finance-summer-2028-timeline/, September 2026; https://www.adventiscg.com/class-of-2029-recruiting-timeline, September 2026; https://www.extern.com/post/finance-internships-summer-2027-guide, updated October 2026)
- Most portals are rolling; "the effective deadline is the day the portal opens." (Source: Prospect Rock July 2026)

## 5. GPA and school facts

- Bank of America postings list GPA as "preferred": 3.5 for Investment Banking (also Global Markets, Research, Technology, Operations), 3.2 for Strategy & Management. (Sources: Prospect Rock July 2026; Extern BofA guide October 2026)
- Goldman Sachs publishes no GPA minimum on its summer analyst postings. (Source: https://www.superdayai.com/blog/goldman-sachs-summer-analyst-2028-application, September 2026)
- Mergers & Inquisitions: from a non-target "you need perfect or near-perfect grades (3.8+ GPA)"; bankers "might be skeptical of a 3.5 GPA from a lesser-known school"; a non-target has roughly 1 to 10 IB placements over five years; no numeric cutoff defines a target. (Source: https://mergersandinquisitions.com/investment-banking-target-schools/, undated, comments through 2026)

(Source: https://www.collegetransitions.com/dataverse/top-feeders-banking, updated April 2025)

## 6. Networking mechanics

- Informational interview emails to the right people get "a 10-25% response rate," and about half of responders take a call; gather 20 to 30 names; expect a few hundred emails from a target or 1,000 to 2,000 from a non-target; aim for 2 to 3 calls a week. (Source: https://mergersandinquisitions.com/investment-banking-networking/, undated, comments through August 2024)
- An informational interview is a 10 to 15 minute call about the banker's career and advice, requested in five sentences or fewer, with 2 to 3 follow-ups and a later "mini-ask" for a referral; start 6 to 12 months before recruiting; budget 300 to 500 emails over about six months. (Source: https://mergersandinquisitions.com/investment-banking-informational-interviews/, undated)
- "Junior bankers respond at far higher rates than MDs." (Source: Prospect Rock July 2026)
- How referrals enter bank applicant tracking systems: no bank page found. UNVERIFIED. Centerview's page discourages emailing multiple people at the firm (section 2).

## 7. Interview process

- Goldman Sachs, official: "your first step is a 30-minute video meeting" on HireVue (HackerRank for engineers); final rounds are "between two and five interviews for campus hires, depending on the division." (Sources: https://www.goldmansachs.com/careers/students, seen; https://www.goldmansachs.com/careers/students/prepare, seen)
- Goldman HireVue detail, secondary: 4 to 6 recorded questions, 30 seconds prep, about 2 minutes per answer; invitations expire in about a week. (Source: https://www.joinleland.com/library/a/goldman-sachs-internship-acceptance-rate-superday-prep-and-tips, June 2026)
- Morgan Stanley IB SA: application (rank up to three IBD groups and three offices), mandatory HireVue (mostly behavioral), first round mixing STAR and technicals, then a Superday with analysts through MDs; Leland says 3 to 5 interviews. Lazard: HireVue, 30 to 45 minute first round, Superday of 4 to 6 back-to-back 30 to 45 minute interviews. (Sources: Extern Morgan Stanley and Lazard guides; https://www.joinleland.com/library/a/morgan-stanley-internship-guide-process-questions-and-tips, July 2025)
- A typical Superday is "four to six back-to-back sessions" (Prospect Rock); Wall Street Prep calls it the final step, "back-to-back interviews with bankers ranging from analysts, associates, and senior bankers," occasionally "well over 10+" short ones. (Sources: Prospect Rock July 2026; https://www.wallstreetprep.com/knowledge/superday/, updated January 2024)
- Technical categories: accounting (three-statement links, working capital), equity vs enterprise value, multiples and the three valuation methods, DCF (unlevered FCF, WACC, terminal value), then M&A and LBO as lower priority; Prospect Rock adds trading and transaction comps and "increasingly LBO fundamentals." (Sources: https://mergersandinquisitions.com/investment-banking-interview-questions-and-answers/, undated; https://www.wallstreetprep.com/knowledge/finance-interview-questions-and-answers/, updated January 2023; Prospect Rock July 2026)

## 8. Conversion facts

- No bank publishes a return-offer rate. Community-reported: Goldman about 70 to 80 percent in leaner years to 90 percent-plus recently (Extern, citing WSO and Reddit); Bank of America roughly 70 to 85 percent (Extern); Morgan Stanley IB about 60 to 70 percent (Leland, unsourced). (Sources: Extern Goldman and BofA guides; Leland Morgan Stanley guide)
- Cross-industry benchmark: NACE intern-to-full-time conversion 63.1 percent for 2024-25 interns, as cited by Extern. (Source: Extern Summer 2027 guide above)
- Full-time recruiting timeline for non-converters (August to October of senior fall): no source retrieved. UNVERIFIED.

## 9. Pay

- First-year analyst base at large banks $100,000 to $125,000 (25th to 75th percentile), total $165,000 to $225,000, bonuses 100 percent cash; elite boutique Year 1 total about $180,000 to $195,000; reflects end-2025 bonuses. (Source: https://mergersandinquisitions.com/investment-banker-salary/, "2026 Update," seen)
- Summer Analyst pay: Morgan Stanley IB $52.89 per hour, about $110,000 annualized, per official postings (Extern); Leland says $110,000 to $120,000 annualized, about $9,000 to $10,000 a month over 10 weeks; Bank of America front office about $110,000 pro-rated (community-labeled). (Sources: Extern Morgan Stanley and BofA guides; Leland July 2025)
- A firm-page $110,000 bulge-bracket first-year base was not retrieved (postings are not linkable); consistent with M&I's range but UNVERIFIED against a primary page.
