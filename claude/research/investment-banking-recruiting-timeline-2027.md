Research date: October 11, 2026. Scope: Summer 2028 IB Summer Analyst cycle (Class of 2029), with the Summer 2027 cycle as the evidence base. WebSearch budget ran out mid-session; thinly sourced items are flagged.

**Headline finding the article must handle:** the Summer 2028 cycle is already open. Evercore's 2028 Summer Analyst postings (NY Generalist, Chicago, Houston, Menlo Park, SF, Richmond, Toronto, Restructuring, ECM) opened September 9, 2026 and close November 15, 2026 (Source: https://evercore.tal.net/candidate/jobboard/vacancy/2/adv/, Oct 2026). Bulge brackets are expected December 2026 to January 2027. "Apply in calendar 2027" holds only for the bulge-bracket and later middle-market waves.

## 3. News coverage on timeline shifts, 2024 to 2026

- Vault, Dec 22, 2025: "students are now expected to apply for banking internships nearly two years in advance." GS, JPM, PJT had not posted 2027 roles by mid-December 2025 (Source: VLT).
- eFinancialCareers, Dec 12, 2025: "there is a general trend to recruit earlier and earlier," but "it doesn't seem to have reached these extremes at the biggest banks quite yet" (Source: EFC).
- Fortune, Jun 6, 2025: banks are "recruiting before students have declared their major"; JPM analysts can now reach associate in 2.5 years instead of 3 (Source: https://fortune.com/2025/06/06/jamie-dimon-jpmorgan-fire-talent-who-accept-roles-within-18-months).
- Dimon, Georgetown, Sep 2024: "you take a job at a private equity shop before you even start with us... I think that's unethical" (Source: Fortune above; Banking Dive https://www.bankingdive.com/news/apollo-general-atlantic-jpmorgan-interview-offer-private-equity-investment-bank-analysts-2027/750527/, Jun 12, 2025).
- Background: Poets&Quants, Mar 6, 2017, documents the move from junior-fall to sophomore-spring; Kelley's Susie Clarke: "The demand for top talent is starting earlier and earlier" (Source: https://poetsandquantsforundergrads.com/first-jobs/bulge-bracket-banks-seeking-earlier-access-undergrad-biz-majors/).
- No 2026 report found that banks delayed or standardized SA opens. The only observed 2026 shift is earlier (Evercore to September). UNVERIFIED beyond that.

## 4. PE on-cycle timing and JPMorgan's 2025 policy

- JPM memo, dated Jun 4, 2025, from Filippo Gori and John Simmons, co-heads of global banking, to incoming US analysts: "If you accept a position with another company before joining us or within your first 18 months... you will be provided notice and your employment with the firm will end" (Source: Fortune, Jun 6, 2025, above; date per https://ibinterviewquestions.com/blog/pe-on-cycle-recruiting-banks-crackdown-2026, May 2026). Banking Dive quotes the memo: "Avoiding potential conflicts of interest is crucial to maintaining the trust and confidence our clients place in us" (Source: Banking Dive, Jun 12, 2025).
- Apollo letter, Jun 11, 2025 (David Sambur, Nicole Bonsignore): "we will not formally interview and extend offers this year for the class of 2027." General Atlantic, Jun 12, 2025: "does not plan to conduct formal interviews or extend offers this year for the Associate Class of 2027." Marc Rowan: recruiting "has crept earlier and earlier every year" (Source: Banking Dive, Jun 12, 2025). TPG also paused (Source: https://www.aol.com/articles/private-equity-hit-pause-junior-105801682.html, Business Insider, Dec 30, 2025).
- Goldman, Jul 9, 2025 (Bloomberg via eFC): IB juniors to certify every three months that they have not accepted jobs elsewhere; spokesperson: "We are committed to a culture where our employees act with integrity" (Source: https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/goldman-sachs-oath, Jul 2025).
- Prior on-cycle starts: Jun 2023 and Jun 2024, the latter "the earliest on-cycle kickoff in history" (Source: https://protectedsaturdays.beehiiv.com/p/oncycle-2026, Aug 2024).
- Restart: Monday Jan 5, 2026. Blackstone, Apollo, Carlyle, TPG, Silver Lake, General Atlantic, H&F, Warburg interviewing by Jan 6; one analyst interviewed at 7:30am and accepted a job starting 20 months later by 9pm (FT via https://www.efinancialcareers.com/news/private-equity-interviews, Jan 7, 2026; https://www.privateequitywire.co.uk/private-equity-firms-restart-delayed-graduate-hiring-after-recruitment-backlash/, Jan 7, 2026). Apollo said on-cycle filled "less than half" of its seats (Source: ibinterviewquestions, May 2026).

## 5. HireVue and first-round mechanics

- Goldman: HireVue follows the resume screen; invitations "expire about a week after receipt" (Source: https://www.extern.com/post/goldman-sachs-internship-guide, Oct 2026). WSO posters in Jan 2026 reported invites within hours to about two days of applying, arriving in waves, one attempt per question (Source: WSO GS mega thread, Jan 2026, crowd-sourced).
- JPM: 3-5 mostly behavioral questions, 30 seconds prep, 90-120 seconds per answer, one retry; no published lag (Source: Extern JPM guide, Oct 2026).
- Morgan Stanley: HireVue mandatory, typically due about two days after the application deadline (Source: Extern MS guide, Oct 2026).
- General: video and test deadlines are "3-to-7-day completion" windows (Source: PRP, Jul 2026; EXT, Oct 2026). Evercore and Moelis use live first rounds, no HireVue (Extern Evercore guide; Moelis careers page).

## 6. Internship dates, return offers, full-time recruiting

- Length: GS 9-10 weeks; JPM IB 9 weeks; MS about 10; Evercore and Harris Williams 10 weeks, June to August; Piper Sandler June to August 2027 (Sources: Extern GS, JPM, MS, Evercore guides, Oct 2026; Harris Williams and Piper Sandler pages above).
- Return offers: often extended in the final week or shortly after; sometimes delayed to early fall (Source: https://ibinterviewquestions.com/blog/investment-banking-recruiting-timeline-guide, updated Oct 2026). No bank publishes a rate; community estimates of 70-90% are UNVERIFIED.
- Full-time without a return offer: postings open Sep-Oct of senior year, interviews Oct-Dec, offers through fall (Sources: ibinterviewquestions above; https://www.joinleland.com/library/a/investment-banking-recruiting, Sep 2025). WSO's "FT 2027 Application Megathread" was active in Oct 2026 (Source: https://www.wallstreetoasis.com/forum/investment-banking).
