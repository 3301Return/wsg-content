## Program-name corrections

- **McKinsey Insight is NOT a sophomore program.** It's the early-exposure program for advanced professional degree (APD) candidates: PhDs, postdocs, MDs. 2026 cycle: interest form due Mar 25, program Apr 30–May 2 in Chicago, early Associate application due May 4. McKinsey's sophomore program is the **Sophomore Summer Business Analyst (SSBA)** internship. Source: mckinsey.com/careers/students/insight/overview.
- **Bridge to BCG is NOT a sophomore program** either — also for advanced-degree candidates. BCG's sophomore internship is **Growing Future Leaders (GFL)**.
- **BCG Advance changed audience:** the 2026 edition targeted JUNIORS (grad Dec 2026–Jul 2027), open to "all fields of study and backgrounds" — no longer the sophomore-women program older sources describe. Invite-only half-day conference, no interview attached.
- **BCG "Bridge to Consulting"** (freshman/sophomore series) reported not running for 2026.
- **Bain's sophomore program is BEL (Building Entrepreneurial Leaders)** — no "Bain Build" program found. BEL is a 1.5-day exploratory program (unpaid, 14 offices), NOT an internship. 2026 cycle dropped identity-based eligibility ("high-achieving sophomore/second year students"). Bain's full sophomore suite: **BEL + CREW (sophomore women, ~1.5-day + mentor pairing, reported ACI interview opportunity) + First Forward (first-gen/lower-SES) + BASE (week-long BASE Camp)**. All at bain.com/careers/work-with-us/internships-programs/.
