# Consulting Recruiting Timeline (Undergrad)

This file is the source of truth for any WSG consulting article that references recruiting years, deadlines, or eligibility. Read it in full before writing any sentence with a year. The consulting cycle differs from the IB cycle in important ways — most importantly, the standard consulting summer-internship application is a **junior-summer** model, not the sophomore-summer model that BB IB uses.

## The standard cycle (junior summer)

The default undergrad consulting recruiting path:

`application year A` → `junior summer internship year A` → `graduation year A + 1`

Note the difference from IB: IB applications happen the year BEFORE the internship (sophomores apply in year A for Summer A+1 internship). Consulting applications happen IN THE SAME CALENDAR YEAR as the internship — juniors apply in spring of their junior year for that same year's summer internship.

| Cycle name | Apply | Intern | Graduate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2026 cycle | Spring 2026 | Summer 2026 | Spring 2027 |
| 2027 cycle | Spring 2027 | Summer 2027 | Spring 2028 |
| 2028 cycle | Spring 2028 | Summer 2028 | Spring 2029 |

**Eligibility framing for an article about the 2026 cycle:** "rising juniors / juniors at the time of internship, graduating Spring 2027" — Class of 2027.

## Sophomore-year affinity programs

MBB and most tier-2 firms run separate, smaller programs for sophomores designed to identify diverse candidates early. The Class of 2028 example:

| Program | Open to | Apply | Intern |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| McKinsey Insight | Sophomores (Class of 2028) | Fall/Winter 2025-26 | Summer 2026 (sophomore summer) |
| BCG Bridge to BCG | Sophomores (Class of 2028) | Fall/Winter 2025-26 | Summer 2026 |
| Bain Build (formerly BEL) | Sophomores (Class of 2028) | Fall/Winter 2025-26 | Summer 2026 |

These programs typically lead to fast-tracked offers for the standard junior-summer cycle the following year. They are MUCH smaller than the main junior-summer class — typically 30-60 sophomores per program per firm in the US.

**The 60% rule.** Industry reporting indicates more than 60% of interview slots are filled from the first application deadline. Apply in the first window, not the second.

## Interview structure (MBB and most tier-2)

Three-round standard:

- **Round 1 — pre-interview assessment.** McKinsey Solve (gamified), BCG Casey (chatbot), Bain Online Test or SOVA. Pass/fail. Required.
- **Round 2 — first-round interviews.** Typically two interviews: one case + one behavioral, OR one combined case-and-behavioral with two interviewers across 60-90 minutes total. Conducted 4-6 weeks after the application deadline.
- **Round 3 — final round / "Decision Round."** Typically two cases + a partner conversation. The partner conversation isn't just behavioral — partners assess judgment, polish, and fit.

Most candidates who get to the final round have offers extended within 1-2 weeks of the partner conversation.

## Why consulting recruiting moved up

The June 2024 update from Columbia Career Education and the 2025-26 timeline shift reflect two pressures:

1. **MBB cross-firm competition.** Each firm wants to lock candidates before peers extend competing offers. Moving deadlines up traps high-performers earlier.
2. **MBB vs IB competition for the same target-school candidates.** IB's on-cycle compression (Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, MS recruiting first-year analysts within 6 months of starting) has spilled into the undergrad recruiting calendar. MBB recruiting started moving up in response.

## Application materials

- **Resume.** One page. Quantified achievements. Strong GPA disclosure (3.7+ preferred). For non-business majors, lean on leadership and quantitative coursework.
- **Cover letter.** Required at McKinsey and Bain; often optional at BCG. Write one cover letter per firm — generic letters get cut.
- **Transcript.** Required at most firms. Confirm requirement before submission.

## Networking before the application

Different from IB. Consulting firms officially say networking does not affect application outcomes. In practice, named contacts at the firm — through coffee chats, recruiting events, school-specific employer pages (e.g., bain.com/careers/meet-us/campus/ac-schools/{your school}) — give the candidate a meaningful boost on borderline applications.

The right cadence: 1-2 coffee chats per target firm during fall of sophomore year, then again in fall of junior year. Mention the contact in the cover letter.

## Affinity / pipeline programs to know

- **McKinsey Insight** — sophomores. Diverse identification. Strong pipeline into BA offers.
- **BCG Bridge to BCG** — same model.
- **Bain Build** — same model.
- **Bain Building Entrepreneurial Leaders (BEL)** — older name for Bain Build, still seen in some materials.
- **McKinsey Forward** — open self-paced learning program (not invite-only). Useful for non-target candidates wanting credibility on their resume.
- **MBB Sophomore Summer Programs** for diverse candidates — these are the affinity programs above. Worth naming explicitly so readers don't miss them.

## Tier-2 and Big 4 strategy timeline (informal)

Tier 2 firms (OW, Strategy&, Kearney, L.E.K., Roland Berger, Accenture Strategy) and Big 4 strategy practices (Monitor Deloitte, EY-Parthenon) generally run their main application windows 2-6 weeks behind MBB. Many tier-2 firms run rolling applications through summer/fall. A candidate who misses MBB deadlines still has real shots at tier 2 if they move within a month.

## Economic consulting timeline

Analysis Group, Cornerstone, NERA, Compass Lexecon, Bates White all run junior-summer internship recruiting on cycles aligned with MBB. Deadlines vary firm-by-firm. Most run fall-of-junior-year application for the following summer.

## How to write recruiting-timeline claims in articles

**Always reference the cycle by year, not by season.** "The 2026 cycle" or "Summer 2026 internship" — not "this cycle" or "next summer."

**Always state who is eligible.** "Rising juniors at the time of internship (Class of 2027)" — be explicit about the graduation year.

**Always include the [VERIFY: ...] tag on any deadline.** Deadlines shift cycle to cycle.

**Cite a university career-center post as the canonical source.** Tufts career, MIT Sloan CDO, Columbia Career Ed, Dartmouth Career Design, Yale OCS. These pages are evergreen and authoritative. Avoid linking to MBB blog posts or third-party sites with rotating posting URLs.
