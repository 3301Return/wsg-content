# Tier 2 Strategy Firms

This tier sits one rung below MBB in prestige and class-size visibility but hires comparable analyst classes, runs the same case-interview style, and has comparable PE / corp-dev / tech-PM exits. WSG readers in the bottom 80% of the GPA distribution at targets, or strong candidates at non-targets, should treat tier 2 as the primary landing zone, not the consolation prize. Several of these firms place better into specific industries than MBB does (Oliver Wyman in FIG, LEK in healthcare, Roland Berger in industrials).

## Oliver Wyman (OW)

- **Owned by:** Marsh McLennan (publicly traded, NYSE: MMC).
- **Founded:** 1984, merged with Mercer Management Consulting in 2007 to form current entity.
- **Headcount:** ~7,000 globally.
- **Undergrad role title:** Consultant. Summer version is Summer Consultant.
- **Strongest practice areas:** Financial Services (the deepest non-FIG-boutique FS practice in consulting), Actuarial, Risk, Transportation, Health & Life Sciences, Aerospace & Defense.
- **Case style:** Closer to BCG/Bain — candidate-led. Quantitative emphasis is high. Be ready for a tough mental-math round.
- **Comp parity:** Within 5-10% of MBB base. Bonus structure similar.
- **Distinguishing feature:** OW Financial Services pays a meaningful premium over the rest of the firm and over MBB practice-area equivalents. The FS recruiting track is essentially separate.
- **Best for:** Candidates with a focused interest in financial services strategy, asset management, insurance, or actuarial work. OW is the strongest non-MBB option in those verticals.

## Strategy& (PwC)

- **Owned by:** PwC (acquired Booz & Company in 2014).
- **Founded:** 1914, Edwin G. Booz. Originally Booz Allen Hamilton commercial practice, separated to Booz & Company in 2008, acquired by PwC in 2014.
- **Headcount:** ~3,500 dedicated strategy consultants within the PwC umbrella.
- **Undergrad role title:** Associate. Summer Associate.
- **Strongest practice areas:** Deals (M&A), Digital, Operations, Customer, Energy/Utilities.
- **Case style:** Standard strategy-firm case interview. Less brainteaser-heavy than BCG.
- **Comp parity:** Within 5-10% of MBB base.
- **Distinguishing feature:** Strategy& deal-advisory work is closely paired with PwC's deals practice, giving a clearer M&A-adjacent track than pure strategy firms. Good positioning for candidates aiming at PE or corporate-development exits.

## Kearney (A.T. Kearney)

- **Owned by:** Independent (was EDS's consulting arm, then independent via management buyout in 2006).
- **Founded:** 1926, Andrew Thomas Kearney (originally a McKinsey partner).
- **Headcount:** ~4,500 globally.
- **Undergrad role title:** Business Analyst. Summer Business Analyst.
- **Strongest practice areas:** Operations & Supply Chain (their flagship and the strongest non-MBB operations practice), Strategic Sourcing, Procurement, Industrial Goods.
- **Case style:** Standard.
- **Comp parity:** ~95% of MBB base.
- **Distinguishing feature:** Operations and procurement excellence. The firm's procurement-consulting heritage is the deepest in the industry.

## L.E.K. Consulting

- **Founded:** 1983 by three former Bain partners (Iain Evans, Richard Koch, James Lawrence — initials L.E.K.).
- **Headcount:** ~2,000 globally.
- **Undergrad role title:** Associate. Summer Associate.
- **Strongest practice areas:** Life Sciences (one of the top two life-sciences consulting practices, with ZS), M&A strategy and commercial diligence (very strong PE-side dealflow), Aerospace & Defense, Consumer.
- **Case style:** Quantitative heavy. Cases are typically more data-driven than the average MBB case.
- **Comp parity:** ~95% of MBB base.
- **Distinguishing feature:** Heavy PE deal-diligence work. Analysts get repeated exposure to commercial due diligence, which is essentially the same workflow as a PE associate. Strong positioning for direct exit to PE.

## Roland Berger

- **Headquartered:** Munich. The largest strategy firm of European origin.
- **Founded:** 1967, Roland Berger.
- **Headcount:** ~3,500 globally.
- **Undergrad role title:** Junior Consultant. Internship is Praktikum / Summer Intern.
- **Strongest practice areas:** Automotive (top of the industry), Industrial Goods, Aerospace, Restructuring (the most-developed restructuring practice in European consulting).
- **US presence:** Smaller than the European footprint. New York, Chicago, Boston, Detroit, Houston.
- **Case style:** Standard.
- **Comp parity:** ~90-95% of MBB base in the US, slightly higher relative comp in Europe.
- **Distinguishing feature:** Industrial and automotive specialization. The only strategy firm with serious restructuring credentials.

## Accenture Strategy (Accenture)

- **Owned by:** Accenture (publicly traded, NYSE: ACN).
- **Founded:** 1989 as Andersen Consulting; rebranded Accenture in 2001.
- **Headcount:** Accenture Strategy is ~13,000 within Accenture's broader ~750,000 workforce.
- **Undergrad role title:** Analyst (Strategy Analyst track). Summer Analyst.
- **Strongest practice areas:** Digital transformation, Technology Strategy, Sustainability Strategy.
- **Case style:** Standard, with heavier emphasis on tech / digital cases than MBB.
- **Comp parity:** ~85-95% of MBB base.
- **Distinguishing feature:** Accenture Strategy plus implementation is one of the largest combined offerings in the industry. The trade-off is that "Strategy" inside Accenture has less prestige separation from broader Accenture Consulting than candidates often realize. WSG readers should verify which Accenture track they're being recruited into.

## What "tier 2" actually means in practice

- **Same case-interview style as MBB**, so prep transfers directly. Reps spent for MBB carry over.
- **Slightly broader school targets.** Tier-2 firms generally recruit at a wider list of non-targets than MBB. Strong non-target candidates have a more realistic shot here.
- **Lifestyle is comparable.** Travel, hours, and intensity match MBB at the analyst level.
- **Exit options.** Tier-2 placements into PE, hedge funds, tech PM roles, and HBS/GSB/Wharton MBA programs are roughly comparable to MBB at the 75th-percentile performance level. The brand discount shows up most at the bottom of the performance curve.

## Comp note

All comp figures above are approximate 2026 estimates. Verify against firm career-page postings or recent Management Consulted / Vault salary surveys before using in an article.
