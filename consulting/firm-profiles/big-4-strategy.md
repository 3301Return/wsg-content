# Big 4 Strategy Arms

The Big 4 accounting firms (Deloitte, EY, KPMG, PwC) each operate a dedicated strategy practice that recruits separately from the broader consulting / advisory / audit pipelines. These are real strategy seats — case interviews, MBA-comparable comp, real exits — and they hire materially larger undergrad classes than MBB does. WSG readers who can't crack MBB or tier 2 should treat Big 4 strategy as the next-best landing zone, not as fallback to broader advisory or audit. The boundary between "strategy" and "advisory" inside each Big 4 firm matters: getting into the strategy arm vs the broader consulting arm is a different career trajectory.

## Monitor Deloitte (Deloitte)

- **Origin:** Monitor Group founded 1983 by Mark Fuller and Michael Porter. Acquired by Deloitte out of Chapter 11 bankruptcy in 2013.
- **Position inside Deloitte:** The strategy practice within Deloitte Consulting. Sits above the broader Strategy & Operations (S&O) practice, which is itself one of the largest non-MBB strategy hirers.
- **Undergrad role title:** Business Analyst (Monitor Deloitte BA). Summer Business Analyst.
- **Headcount estimate:** ~2,500-3,500 strategy consultants under the Monitor Deloitte banner globally.
- **Strongest practice areas:** Growth strategy, M&A strategy, Innovation, Customer Strategy, Strategy & Analytics.
- **Comp parity:** ~85-95% of MBB base. [VERIFY]
- **Distinguishing feature:** The Porter strategic heritage (Five Forces, generic strategies, value chains) shows up in the case-prep materials and interviewer language. Strong consumer and retail practice.
- **Watch out:** "Deloitte" recruiting pipelines are confusing. A Deloitte Consulting Business Analyst offer is NOT the same as a Monitor Deloitte BA offer. Confirm which track the candidate is in.

## EY-Parthenon (EY)

- **Origin:** Parthenon Group founded 1991 by two former Bain partners (Bill Achtmeyer, John Rutherford). Acquired by EY in 2014.
- **Position inside EY:** EY-Parthenon is one of EY's three core service lines (Audit, Tax, Consulting) and operates as a distinct strategy practice. EY Strategy & Transactions is the broader umbrella; EY-Parthenon is the strategy specialist arm.
- **Undergrad role title:** Associate. Summer Associate.
- **Headcount estimate:** ~7,500 globally.
- **Strongest practice areas:** Education, Healthcare, Private Equity (commercial due diligence — one of the largest CDD shops in the industry), TMT.
- **Comp parity:** ~95-100% of MBB base. [VERIFY]
- **Distinguishing feature:** Heavy PE diligence dealflow. Like L.E.K., EY-Parthenon analysts get serious commercial due diligence reps. Strong direct exits to PE associate seats.
- **Watch out:** EY Strategy & Transactions is a related-but-different umbrella that includes M&A advisory; the lift in EY-Parthenon comes from working on the Parthenon strategy + CDD work, not the broader S&T pool.

## PwC Strategy& (PwC)

Covered in detail in `tier-2-strategy.md`. PwC's strategy arm is large enough and prestigious enough that most industry observers classify it as tier 2 rather than Big 4 strategy. Including a note here for completeness — if an article structures around "Big 4 strategy practices," include PwC Strategy& in the Big 4 lineup even though we profile it elsewhere as tier 2.

## KPMG Strategy (KPMG)

- **Origin:** KPMG Strategy practice; never had a marquee acquisition like Monitor or Parthenon, so the brand is less established than the other three.
- **Position inside KPMG:** Strategy is a service line within KPMG Advisory.
- **Undergrad role title:** Associate. Summer Associate.
- **Headcount estimate:** Smaller than the other three Big 4 strategy practices; ~1,500-2,500 globally.
- **Strongest practice areas:** Customer & Operations strategy, Deal Strategy (CDD), Restructuring strategy.
- **Comp parity:** ~85-90% of MBB base. [VERIFY]
- **Distinguishing feature:** KPMG Strategy is the least-established of the Big 4 strategy arms. Real strategy work happens but the brand is weaker than Monitor Deloitte or EY-Parthenon. Strong dealflow on the CDD side.

## What "Big 4 strategy" actually means in practice

- **Smaller than the broader advisory practice.** Each Big 4 firm has a 10x or 20x larger consulting practice outside of the strategy arm. The strategy seat is the prestige seat within Big 4; the broader advisory roles are real seats but different career trajectories.
- **Case-interview style is the same as MBB.** Prep transfers directly.
- **MBA sponsorship.** All four Big 4 strategy practices sponsor a portion of high-performers for top-tier MBAs.
- **PE exits.** EY-Parthenon and Monitor Deloitte place into PE associate seats at meaningful rates (comparable to L.E.K. and tier-2 strategy firms). KPMG Strategy and PwC Strategy& place too, with slightly lower deal volume.
- **Class-size leverage.** Big 4 strategy classes are larger than MBB. For a WSG reader with a 3.7 GPA at a non-target, Big 4 strategy is a materially higher-probability target than MBB.

## When to use "Big 4 strategy" vs "Big 4 advisory" in an article

The reader almost certainly conflates the two. Be explicit:

- **Big 4 strategy** = Monitor Deloitte, EY-Parthenon, PwC Strategy&, KPMG Strategy. These are strategy practices that compete with MBB on the same engagement types and use the same case-interview funnel.
- **Big 4 advisory** = the broader consulting / risk / transformation / tech-consulting practices inside each Big 4. Larger by 10-20x but different role profile.

If an article uses "Big 4 strategy" without naming which of the four sub-practices it means, the article is generic. Always name the specific practice.

## The honest sourcing note

Comp, headcount, and brand-prestige rankings shift. Verify each before publishing.
