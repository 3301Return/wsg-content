# Boutique Consulting Firms

"Boutique" in consulting means focus, not size. A boutique firm specializes — by industry vertical, by function, or by methodology — and competes with MBB inside that focus area on a peer level. WSG readers who care about a specific industry (healthcare, TMT, life sciences, financial services) should treat the right boutique as equal or better than MBB for their use case. Several boutiques pay above MBB base, and many of them place into top-tier MBAs and PE seats at MBB-comparable rates.

This file lists the boutiques most relevant to ambitious American undergrads. Not exhaustive — exhaustive would be 200+ firms. These are the ones WSG articles should reference when discussing boutique consulting as a category.

## Healthcare & Life Sciences

### ZS Associates

- **Founded:** 1983, Andris Zoltners and Prabhakant Sinha (Northwestern Kellogg professors).
- **Headcount:** ~13,000 globally — the largest healthcare-focused consulting firm in the world.
- **Undergrad role title:** Business Operations Associate (BOA), Decision Analytics Associate (DAA). Two different tracks.
- **Strongest practice:** Pharma commercial strategy, sales-force sizing and structure, market access, real-world evidence analytics.
- **Comp parity:** ~85-95% of MBB base. Significant variation between BOA and DAA tracks.
- **Why WSG readers care:** ZS is the strongest non-MBB option for pharma/biotech-focused candidates. The Kellogg roots show up in heavy quantitative training.

### Putnam Associates

- **Founded:** 1988, Boston.
- **Headcount:** ~600.
- **Undergrad role title:** Associate.
- **Strongest practice:** Pharma/biotech strategy, R&D portfolio decisions, market entry for new therapeutics.
- **Comp parity:** Competitive with MBB at the analyst level.
- **Why WSG readers care:** Boutique with serious life-sciences strategy reputation. Strong placement into pharma corporate development, biotech business-dev, and PE/VC life sciences seats.

### Clearview Healthcare Partners

- **Founded:** 2007, Boston.
- **Headcount:** ~400.
- **Undergrad role title:** Associate.
- **Strongest practice:** Pharma commercial strategy, market access, M&A diligence in biopharma.
- **Comp parity:** Within 5-10% of MBB base.
- **Why WSG readers care:** Hires undergrads directly. Notable feeder into MBA and pharma industry seats.

### Health Advances

- **Founded:** 1992, Boston.
- **Headcount:** ~250.
- **Undergrad role title:** Analyst.
- **Strongest practice:** Healthcare investor due diligence, medical device strategy, diagnostics.
- **Comp parity:** Below MBB on base but lifestyle is materially lighter.
- **Why WSG readers care:** Strong investor-side dealflow (PE and growth equity diligence). Mentioned explicitly by Dartmouth Career Design as a healthcare-focused recruiter.

### L.E.K. Life Sciences

L.E.K. has a dedicated life-sciences practice that operates with similar profile to the standalone life-science boutiques. Covered in the tier-2 strategy file but worth flagging as an alternative to standalone life-sciences boutiques.

## TMT (Technology, Media, Telecom)

### Altman Solon

- **Founded:** 2016, merger of Altman Vilandrie & Company and Solon Management Consulting.
- **Headcount:** ~400.
- **Undergrad role title:** Associate.
- **Strongest practice:** TMT exclusively. Telecom strategy, media business models, software / SaaS strategy, PE diligence for TMT deals.
- **Comp parity:** Competitive with MBB at the analyst level.
- **Why WSG readers care:** Mentioned explicitly by Dartmouth Career Design. The largest pure-play TMT consulting firm in the world. Strong PE-side dealflow.

### Stratecast / Frost & Sullivan TMT

Smaller specialist with TMT focus but materially less prestige than Altman Solon. Include only if a list specifically calls for breadth.

## Financial Services Boutiques

### Oliver Wyman Financial Services (covered in tier 2)

Oliver Wyman's FS practice is functionally a financial-services boutique inside a larger firm. WSG articles should mention it whenever they discuss FS-specialized consulting.

### Simon-Kucher

- **Founded:** 1985, Bonn, Germany.
- **Headcount:** ~2,000 globally.
- **Undergrad role title:** Associate.
- **Strongest practice:** Pricing and revenue strategy across industries; particularly strong in software, healthcare, and financial services pricing.
- **Comp parity:** Within 10-15% of MBB base.
- **Why WSG readers care:** The leading specialist in pricing strategy. Niche but well-respected.

## Marketing & Brand Strategy

### Prophet

- **Founded:** 1992, David Aaker (UC Berkeley).
- **Headcount:** ~700.
- **Undergrad role title:** Associate Consultant.
- **Strongest practice:** Brand strategy, customer experience, growth strategy.
- **Comp parity:** Below MBB base.
- **Why WSG readers care:** The leading brand-strategy boutique. Marketing-focused candidates should know this name.

## Pure Strategy Boutiques

### Innosight

- **Founded:** 2000 by Clayton Christensen (HBS, "innovator's dilemma" framework).
- **Headcount:** ~150.
- **Strongest practice:** Disruptive innovation strategy, corporate venture strategy.
- **Why WSG readers care:** Christensen brand. Small, very selective. Heavy emphasis on innovation frameworks.

### Strategic Decisions Group (SDG)

- **Founded:** 1981.
- **Strongest practice:** Decision analysis, real-options strategy. Niche.
- **Why WSG readers care:** The leading decision-analysis boutique. Mentioned in the Vault guide as the firm of choice for real-options work.

### AlphaSights (knowledge-search adjacent)

- **Founded:** 2008.
- **Headcount:** ~1,500.
- **Note:** Not strictly a strategy consulting firm — AlphaSights does expert-network "knowledge search," helping PE/HF/consulting clients connect with subject-matter experts. WSG articles should mention this only when the article is specifically about expert networks or alt-consulting paths.

## Operations / Procurement Boutiques

### A.T. Kearney (covered in tier 2)

Kearney's procurement and operations heritage means it functions as a procurement-specialist firm inside a larger strategy firm.

### Maine Pointe

- **Strongest practice:** Supply chain and operations consulting.
- **Why WSG readers care:** Niche operations boutique. Mention only in operations-focused articles.

## What "boutique" means for the WSG reader

- **Industry depth > MBB breadth in the focus vertical.** For a candidate who already knows they want pharma, ZS or Putnam is a stronger career start than McKinsey's healthcare practice.
- **Smaller class sizes.** Boutique recruiting is more personalized. A candidate who interviews well at a boutique can land an offer with materially less networking polish than MBB requires.
- **Different exit profile.** Boutique exits trend industry-specific (pharma corp dev for life-sciences boutiques, telco strategy for TMT boutiques). MBB exits trend brand-name-prestige (PE, top-tier tech PM, MBA, F500 strategy).
- **Compensation generally below MBB but lifestyle generally lighter.** True for most healthcare and TMT boutiques; not true for the OW FS / L.E.K. / EY-Parthenon CDD tracks where comp is at or above MBB.

## The honest selection note

This list is curated, not exhaustive. The Vault guide lists hundreds more. WSG articles should pull from this list and add others only when a clear specialist reputation justifies the addition. If an article wants to list more than 5 boutiques, run a freshness check on the niche (e.g., "best healthcare consulting boutiques 2026") before publishing to confirm no major new entrant has displaced the firms on this list.
