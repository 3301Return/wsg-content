## Linking guidelines for WSG articles

**Always link to:** firm career pages, university career-center posts.

**Sometimes link to:** Management Consulted, PrepLounge, Hacking the Case Interview, IGotAnOffer, Crafting Cases — third-party industry blogs that are credibly written.

**Never link to:** rotating job-posting URLs (Greenhouse `gh_jid=`, Workday job IDs, iCIMS specific postings, LinkedIn job postings). Same rule as the IB content. See `style-guide/link-verification.md`.

**Avoid linking to:** Reddit threads, generic SEO blog farms, paid case-prep program landing pages (unless the article is specifically reviewing one).
