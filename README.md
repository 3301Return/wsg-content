# WallStreetGuide.net Article Generator

A repeatable system for generating SEO-optimized blog articles in the WSG voice — ultimate guides, listicles, and niche posts.

## How to use it (the 30-second version)

1. **Set up samples** (one-time): drop 2-3 of your existing best WSG articles into `samples/ultimate-guides/`, `samples/listicles/`, and `samples/niche/`.
2. **Set up your calendar** (one-time): drop your keyword + headline plan into `content-calendar/`.
3. **Optional: write a deep dive** for any topic you'll spawn multiple articles from (e.g., `deep-dives/restructuring.md`).
4. **Ask for an article** in chat: *"Write a WSG ultimate guide on how to break into restructuring, target keyword: how to break into restructuring."*
5. Claude loads the skill, reads the style guide, picks the right template, drafts to `drafts/draft-{slug}-v1.md`.

## What to do before your first article

1. **Drop in samples.** Put 2-3 of your best published WSG articles into the matching `samples/{format}/` folder. Markdown files with the article body, or `.docx` files, or even just `.md` files containing the URL — any of those work. The generator pulls voice and structure from these.

2. **Drop in your content calendar.** Put your keyword + headline plan into `content-calendar/calendar.md` (or a spreadsheet). The generator will use this to enforce exact headlines and pull the right keyword for each post.

3. **Write your first deep dive (optional but powerful).** If you have a topic you'll spawn multiple articles from — restructuring, M&A process, IB recruiting timeline — write the comprehensive internal guide as `deep-dives/{topic}.md`. The deep dive is just for you and Claude. Articles are spawned from it.

## How to ask for articles

### Casual (most common)
> "Write a WSG ultimate guide on how to break into restructuring. Target keyword: how to break into restructuring. Anchor it in `deep-dives/restructuring.md`."

The skill will infer the brief from your message and ask only for what's truly missing before drafting.

### Structured (when you want full control)
1. Copy `templates/_brief-template.md` to `briefs/brief-{slug}.md`
2. Fill in every field (headline, format, keyword, target reader, deep dive source, specific scenarios to include, hooks, items to avoid)
3. Tell Claude: *"Write the WSG article from `briefs/brief-{slug}.md`."*

### From the calendar
> "Write the next post in the calendar."

Claude will read `content-calendar/calendar.md`, pick the next pending headline, gather any missing brief fields, and start.

## The deep-dive workflow (your restructuring pattern)

This is the workflow you described — write a comprehensive guide on a topic, then spawn many articles from it.

1. Write `deep-dives/restructuring.md` once. Include: what RX is, how deals work, players, recruiting, comp, day-to-day, exits, common myths, named scenarios.
2. Ask: *"Write a WSG niche article on Evercore restructuring, anchored in `deep-dives/restructuring.md`."*
3. Claude reads the deep dive in full, pulls the slice relevant to Evercore RX, uses the named scenarios from the deep dive as credibility moments, drafts the article.
4. Spawn another: *"Now write an ultimate guide on RX recruiting timeline using the same deep dive."*

One deep dive, many articles. Each article reads like real experience because it's anchored in your specifics.

## Revisions

When you want to revise a draft:

> "In `drafts/draft-{slug}-v1.md`, rewrite section 3 to be harder-hitting and add a specific scenario from the deep dive."

Claude saves a new version as `draft-{slug}-v2.md` (never overwrites).

## When you publish

Move the final draft from `drafts/` to `published/`, dropping the `draft-` prefix and version. Future articles will pull from `published/` as samples — so the more you publish, the better the generator gets at sounding like you.

## Want to evolve the system?

Edit any of these files and the next article will reflect the change automatically:

- `style-guide/style-guide.md` — when your voice or rules evolve
- `style-guide/seo-checklist.md` — when SEO targets change
- `templates/{format}.md` — when you want to tweak what each format includes
- `.claude-skill/SKILL.md` — when you want to change the generation workflow itself

No code changes. The system is just a folder of plain Markdown.
