## What undergrads actually search for in consulting

The Ahrefs pull settled three things:

1. **Strategy consulting is the dominant career-path query.** `strategy consulting` (3,900 vol / KD 7), `strategy consultant` (1,000 / KD 3), `what is strategy consulting` (300 / KD 3 / **TP 3,000**), `top strategy consulting firms` (150 / KD 3). Total cluster: ~5,400/mo of career-relevant search.

2. **Tech consulting is real but split between recruiting and B2B intent.** `tech consulting` (2,000 / KD 4) and `technology consulting` (3,000 / KD 11) are career-relevant. But `IT consulting` (19,000 / KD 12), `digital transformation consulting` (5,700 / KD 5), `AI consulting` (6,900 / KD 49), and `sustainability consulting` (2,300 / KD 43) are mostly B2B intent — companies hiring consultants, not undergrads. **Skip those four despite the volume.**

3. **The single biggest opportunity is generic consulting-recruiting queries**, not vertical-specific ones. `consulting jobs` (5,100 / KD 0), `consulting internships` (2,100 / KD 14), `how to become a consultant` (1,600 / KD 3 / **TP 6,200**), `consulting salary` (900 / KD 0). Total: ~8,300/mo at Score 5 relevance and rankable KD. **This is the biggest single SEO bucket WSG has surfaced in any vertical to date.**

## The Recruiting Relevance Score (1-5)

Per `.claude-skill/recruiting-relevance.md`:

- **Score 5** — direct recruiting content. Ship first.
- **Score 4** — firm career guides, comparisons, rankings, salary. Ship next.
- **Score 3** — career-path explainer (conditional ship if KD ≤ 10).
- **Score 2** — practitioner / B2B content. Default skip.
- **Score 1** — pure definition / instrument explainers. Always skip.

Composite formula: `(volume / max(1, KD)) × recruiting_relevance`. Sort descending.

## Strategic notes

**The "consulting jobs" article is the single biggest SEO opportunity in WSG's keyword universe.** 5,100 monthly searches at KD 0 with direct undergrad-recruiting intent. The closest competitor is generic job-board SERP results (Indeed, LinkedIn, Glassdoor), none of which provide structured undergrad guidance. WSG can own this query with an undergrad-framed listicle that names specific bulge bracket / MBB / tier-2 / Big 4 consulting jobs.

**The strategy consulting cluster (Score 3) is bigger than the strategy consulting firms cluster (Score 4) by volume.** Write the strategy-consulting career guide first, then use it as a hub article for the firm-specific career guides (already covered by the prior `Top 10 Management Consulting Firms` article).

**Tech consulting is undervalued by WSG's current content.** None of the 8 articles written so far cover tech consulting as a career path. Given the 2,000-3,000 monthly volume and KD ≤ 11, this is the highest-leverage NEW vertical for WSG to add.

**Big 4 consulting (2,700 / KD 7) is the most-underserved high-volume keyword for undergrads.** Most undergrad-facing consulting content treats Big 4 as a fallback; WSG can position Big 4 as a real career destination with sourced upside (5-year exit data, MBA sponsorship rates, PE conversion rates) and own the SERP.

**Healthcare consulting was a 5,650-monthly-volume vertical that the user de-prioritized.** That volume reallocates to broad consulting where the audience overlap with WSG mentorship is stronger.
