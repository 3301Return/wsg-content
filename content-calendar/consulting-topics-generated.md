## How to read this file

- **Volume** = US monthly searches per Ahrefs
- **KD** = Keyword Difficulty 0-100. WSG's current Ahrefs footprint is essentially zero, so we target **KD ≤ 15** as the practical ceiling. Above 25 is unrealistic for the next 12 months.
- **TP** = Traffic Potential. The total monthly traffic the #1 ranking page draws across all the keywords it ranks for. Big TP = ranking #1 unlocks a much larger pool than the headline keyword suggests.
- **Tier S** = high volume + low KD + strong WSG fit (ship first)
- **Tier A** = solid volume, very low KD, sophomore/recruiting focus (ship after S)
- **Tier B** = lower volume but underserved and easy to win
- **Already written** = exists in the May 25 article batch; included for cross-reference
