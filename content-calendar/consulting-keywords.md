## Editorial rules for headlines (May 25, 2026)

These rules apply to **all** WSG articles, not just consulting. Treat them as hard rules.

### Rule 1: Keep headlines under 8-10 words

Long headlines lose ranking in Google SERPs and lose clicks in OG cards. Aim for 6-9 words. Cut sub-clauses ("(And How to Land an Offer)" types).

### Rule 2: Lead with opportunity or value, not abstract opinion

Bad: "Why You Shouldn't Overlook Boutique Consulting Firms (And How to Land an Offer)" — abstract opinion, no opportunity signal.

Good: "10 Boutique Consulting Firms to Apply To" — names the opportunity.

Good: "Ultimate Guide to Get Into Boutique Consulting" — names the value (the how).

The headline should answer either: **"What set of opportunities does this give me?"** OR **"How do I get to a specific outcome?"** If the headline does neither, rewrite.

### Rule 3: Avoid college-essay framing

Bad: "Beyond the Case: How to Master Consulting Fit Interview Questions" — the "Beyond the Case:" colon-phrase reads like an academic paper.

Good: "How to Master Consulting Fit Interview Questions" — directly states the value.

Bad: "From Panic to Passed: How to Prepare for Your First Case Study Interview" — same problem.

Good: "How to Prepare for Your First Case Interview" — clean and direct.

Bad: "The Insider Blueprint: Exactly How to Get Into McKinsey as an Undergraduate" — "Insider Blueprint" is fluff.

Good: "How to Get Into McKinsey as an Undergraduate" — direct.

### Rule 4: Describe the value quickly

The headline + the first line of the meta description should together tell the reader exactly what they get. Never make them guess.

## How to use this plan

1. **Pick a headline from a bucket** that matches the article the user is requesting. Use the approved (right-column) version, not the original.
2. **Add the article to the brief** with the approved headline locked in as `headline:` in the frontmatter.
3. **Confirm the format** in the third column. Most consulting articles in this plan are ultimate guides or listicles — niche format is only for firm comparisons (Big 4 vs MBB, BCG vs Bain vs McKinsey).
4. **Cross-reference `deep-dives/consulting/INDEX.md`** to see if a parent deep dive exists. If it does, read it before drafting. If it doesn't, either spawn the deep dive first or pull from `consulting/firm-profiles/` and `consulting/case-interview-frameworks.md`.
5. **Apply the May 25 headline rules to any new consulting headline** that comes up outside this plan. The four rules are universal.
