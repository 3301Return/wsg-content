## How to read this file

- **Vol** = US monthly searches per Ahrefs
- **KD** = Keyword Difficulty 0-100. WSG ceiling is 15.
- **RR** = Recruiting Relevance (1-5). See `.claude-skill/recruiting-relevance.md`.
- **F/S** = Freshman/Sophomore Fit (1-5). Higher = stronger fit for WSG's primary audience.
- **Composite** = `(volume / max(1, KD)) × recruit_rel × (fs_fit / 5)`. The single ranking number.
- **Vertical:** RX = Restructuring, PC = Private Credit, CON = Broad Consulting.
