## How to read this file

- **Vol** = US monthly searches per Ahrefs.
- **KD** = Keyword Difficulty 0-100. WSG ceiling is **15** for new content given the site's current domain rating. KD 16-25 is borderline; over 25 is multi-year work.
- **TP** = Traffic Potential — total monthly traffic the #1 ranking page draws across all the keywords it ranks for.
- **Tier S** = high volume + rankable KD + strong WSG fit (ship first).
- **Tier A** = solid volume, very low KD, recruiting-relevant.
- **Tier B** = lower volume but underserved and easy to win.
