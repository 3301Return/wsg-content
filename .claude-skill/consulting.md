# WSG Consulting Addendum

## Openings for consulting articles (October 8, 2026)

Start with what the thing is. Stephen's model is the McKinsey Solve opener: "McKinsey Solve is the gamified pre-interview assessment every McKinsey candidate has to pass before getting a case interview." His fix for the Casey article follows the same shape: "The BCG Casey chatbot interview is the first-round interview for BCG candidates before you go to a case round."

For assessment and interview articles (Solve, Casey, SOVA, PEI, case types): sentence one says what it is and where it sits in the process; the next sentence gives length, format and scoring; the last says what the article covers.

For career and exit articles: one plain fact, the reader's question, the short answer. Stephen's version for consulting exits: "The average tenure for consultants is two years. So where do consultants go after finishing their time? For the most part there are four main areas: private equity, tech, big business, and entrepreneurship." (Verify any tenure figure before using it.)

Never open with what candidates expect, feel or get wrong.

## The consulting recruiting timeline (read this carefully)

The undergrad consulting cycle is DIFFERENT from the IB cycle. Most WSG-reader confusion comes from this.

**MBB main track (junior-summer internship, applied for ~15 months ahead):** apply in the first half of year A (sophomore spring) → intern Summer A+1 (junior summer) → graduate Spring A+2. This changed with the 2026 cycle (corrections log, Aug 21, 2026).

| Cycle | Apply (MBB) | Intern | Graduate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Summer 2027 internships | Jan-Aug 2026 (McKinsey BAI closed Mar 29; BCG Summer Associate closed Jun 2, no fall round; Bain ACI Mar 29 and Aug 31) | Summer 2027 | Spring 2028 (Class of 2028) |
| Summer 2028 internships | Expected Jan-Aug 2027 [VERIFY per firm] | Summer 2028 | Spring 2029 (Class of 2029) |

Tier 2 and Big 4 strategy run later and often rolling (L.E.K. Sep 15, 2026; most others through fall 2026). Name the firm and the internship summer every time rather than assuming one model.

Compare to IB: IB sophomores also apply in year A for Summer A+1. MBB now runs on a similar lead time, but deadlines differ firm by firm, so never borrow an IB date for a consulting article or the reverse.

**Sophomore programs** are separate from the main track:

- **McKinsey Sophomore Summer Business Analyst (SSBA):** ~10-week paid internship in the sophomore summer. Summer 2027 postings appeared late July 2026; US deadline expected ~Oct 2026 [VERIFY: live posting].
- **BCG Growing Future Leaders (GFL):** ~10-week paid sophomore internship; completing it makes the intern eligible for a junior-year internship offer. Expected to open Aug-Sep 2026 with a mid-Sep to Oct deadline [VERIFY].
- **Bain:** Building Entrepreneurial Leaders (BEL) is a ~1.5-day exploratory program, not an internship; CREW (sophomore women), First Forward and BASE round out the suite. There is no program called "Bain Build."
- **McKinsey Insight and Bridge to BCG are NOT sophomore programs.** Both are for advanced-degree candidates. Never present them to undergrads as sophomore pipelines.

## Voice (October 8, 2026)

Same voice as every WSG article: "you" for the reader, "we" for WSG, and light first-person framing where it helps ("In this article, I lay out the main paths people take after consulting"). No first-person McKinsey memories, no credentials, no author introduction, no byline.

## Firm-list defaults

Use these canonical names. Don't substitute or invent.

- **MBB:** McKinsey & Company, Boston Consulting Group (BCG), Bain & Company. After first use, "McKinsey," "BCG" and "Bain" are enough; don't repeat the full parenthetical expansion inside a long sentence (Stephen flagged this in the exits opener).
- **Tier 2 strategy:** Oliver Wyman, Strategy& (PwC), Kearney (A.T. Kearney), L.E.K. Consulting, Roland Berger, Accenture Strategy.
- **Big 4 strategy:** Monitor Deloitte, EY-Parthenon, PwC Strategy& (often classified as tier 2 too), KPMG Strategy.
- **Healthcare and life sciences boutiques:** ZS Associates, Putnam Associates, Clearview Healthcare Partners, Health Advances, L.E.K. Life Sciences.
- **TMT boutique:** Altman Solon.
- **Pricing specialist:** Simon-Kucher.
- **Brand and marketing:** Prophet.
- **Economic consulting:** Analysis Group, Cornerstone Research, NERA Economic Consulting, Compass Lexecon, Bates White, Brattle Group, Charles River Associates (CRA).
- **Innovation / disruption specialist:** Innosight.
- **Decision analysis specialist:** Strategic Decisions Group (SDG).

## Case-interview vocabulary

- **Five canonical case types:** profitability, market entry, M&A, operations / cost reduction, market sizing.
- **MECE** (mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive). Use it when teaching structure, not as filler.
- **Drivers, not frameworks.** Interviewers want custom structure, not memorized templates. Cite Porter's Five Forces ONLY for industry-structure cases.
- **Pre-interview assessments by firm:** McKinsey Solve, BCG Casey, Bain Online Test / SOVA. Spell them out.
- **Personal Experience Interview (PEI).** McKinsey-specific. Three dimensions: personal impact, entrepreneurial drive, inclusive leadership.

## Five tells of a low-credibility consulting article (always avoid)

1. Recommends Porter's Five Forces for every case.
2. Conflates "Big 4 advisory" with "Big 4 strategy."
3. Lists 20+ case-interview frameworks to memorize.
4. Treats MBB as the only credible path.
5. Cites only Victor Cheng or only Case in Point with no firm-published resources.

## Five tells of a high-credibility consulting article (always aim for)

1. Names specific cases by type with one named drill per type.
2. Names specific firms by sub-practice (Oliver Wyman Financial Services, EY-Parthenon Education, ZS Pharma Decision Analytics).
3. References actual cycle dates with VERIFY tags.
4. Distinguishes sophomore programs (SSBA, GFL, BEL/CREW) from the junior-summer main track.
5. Acknowledges that a boutique beats MBB for some candidate profiles (e.g., pharma → ZS or Putnam over McKinsey healthcare).

Same protocol as the main skill.

## Article-format defaults

- **Listicle on consulting firms.** Include at least 1 boutique and 1 econ consulting firm if the count is 10+.
- **Ultimate guide on consulting.** Open per the openings section above. Put the cycle math (when to apply for which summer, firm by firm) in the first H2 sub-question, not the intro.
- **Niche on a specific firm.** Use the firm-profile file as the spine.

## Listicle structure for consulting articles

Same bullet structure as IB listicles: Website, Apply Link (if open), Deadline, Eligibility, Who they hire / why apply, then a 2-3 sentence description.

## Length

Same target as the main skill (October 8, 2026): about 20 to 25 pages in the built .docx before images.

- Ultimate guide: about 5,000 to 6,000 words
- Listicle: about 4,500 to 6,000 words
- Niche: about 4,500 to 5,500 words

Case-interview and framework articles need room to walk a profitability tree or the arithmetic in a market-sizing case. Walk each case type once, fully, with one worked example; give variations in a sentence or two. A case walk-through that skips a step fails the thoroughness pass; a second walk-through of the same case type fails the length cut.
