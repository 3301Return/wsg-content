# Ultimate Guide Template

**Use when:** Insight-led, definitive guides on a major topic. Reader leaves with a clear mental model and a process to follow.
**Examples:** "How Hard Is It to Get Into Investment Banking? 6 Things That Actually Matter" · "How to Break Into Consulting From a Non-Target"
**Word count:** 2,000 to 2,500
**Required:** 1-2 H2 sub-questions after intro · 6-10 insight sections · CTA at end

## Generation rules (for the skill)

- Pull voice and structure from at least one ultimate-guide sample in `samples/ultimate-guides/` or `published/`
- If a relevant `deep-dives/{topic}.md` exists, read it first and ground the insights in those specifics
- Every numbered section must include at least one specific (named firm, real number, named scenario, or first-person experience)
- Bold full sentences only — never random words
- No em-dashes or hyphens as structural punctuation
- Use the target keyword in: H1, meta, first paragraph, at least 2 H2s, naturally throughout
