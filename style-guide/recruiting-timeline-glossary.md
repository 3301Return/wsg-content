# IB Recruiting Timeline Glossary (mandatory reference before writing any recruiting article)

**Read this before writing any article that references application years, internship years, or graduation years.** Conflating these is the most common factual error in WSG drafts and Stephen has flagged it before.

## The three years that are not the same

For any IB Summer Analyst recruiting cycle, three different calendar years are in play. Treat them as three separate variables:

1. **Application year** — the calendar year in which the candidate submits the application and goes through interviews.
2. **Internship year** — the calendar year of the summer the candidate actually interns. Always one year after the application year for the standard cycle.
3. **Graduation year** — the calendar year the candidate graduates. Always one year after the internship year for the standard cycle (junior summer interns graduate the following spring).

The standard relationship for a sophomore going through junior-summer IB recruiting:

| Variable | Year |
|---|---|
| Sophomore academic year | Fall {N-2} to Spring {N-1} |
| Application year | {N-1} (apps typically open December {N-2} and close spring {N-1}) |
| Internship year | Summer {N} |
| Graduation year | Spring {N+1} |

## The on-cycle compression shifts these by some months but not by a year

PE recruiting and EB IB recruiting now opens earlier than the standard cycle. Evercore and several EBs open sophomore-spring (early in calendar year {N-1}) rather than sophomore-fall, but the internship year is still {N}. The compression accelerates *when* in the year applications happen, not which year the internship lands in.

## Sophomore vs junior internships

A small number of programs run **sophomore summer internships** (BofA's Sophomore SA, Apollo Sophomore SA, JPMorgan Launching Leaders). These hire candidates who will intern the summer between their sophomore and junior year (rather than between junior and senior year).

For a sophomore-summer program:

| Variable | Value |
|---|---|
| Application year | {N-1} (apps typically open fall of freshman year, close winter) |
| Sophomore internship year | Summer {N} (between sophomore and junior year) |
| Graduation year | Spring {N+2} (because they're still two academic years from graduating) |

Example: BofA Sophomore Summer Analyst applicants in fall 2026 → intern Summer 2027 → graduate Spring 2029.

## Things to never write without verifying against this glossary

- Any phrase containing "applying in 20XX for Summer 20XX" without confirming the year math.
- Any phrase like "sophomores graduating December 20XX to June 20XX" without confirming the right class year.
- Any phrase like "by the time you intern in 20XX" without confirming internship year.
- Off-cycle and full-time recruiting timelines, which have separate math.

## The check before drafting

Before writing any sentence that includes a recruiting year, ask:

1. Is this referring to the year the candidate **applies**, **interns**, or **graduates**?
2. Does the year I'm writing match what the article headline implies?
3. Does the eligibility language ("graduating between X and Y") match the internship year, not the application year?

If you cannot answer all three, stop and re-derive from the table above.

## Quick reference card (memorize this for any article you write)

**Sophomore-year recruiting for the standard junior-summer cycle:**

application year `A` → internship year `A + 1` → graduation year `A + 2`

**Sophomore-summer programs (rarer):**

application year `A` → sophomore internship year `A + 1` → graduation year `A + 3`
