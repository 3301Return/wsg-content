# samples/

Curated calibration set the WSG skill reads before drafting: a few gold-standard articles per format (listicle, niche, ultimate-guide) chosen for voice and structure.

Provenance note: samples may themselves be AI-written; they live here for calibration only and are not the article library. The canonical archive is `published/`. Keep this folder small — when a newer article sets the standard for its format, swap it in rather than adding on top.
