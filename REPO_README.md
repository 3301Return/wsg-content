# wsg-content

The content production system for [WallStreetGuide.net](https://wallstreetguide.net) (WSG): editorial style guide, article templates, writer profiles, deep-dive source material, the Claude article-generation skill, QC tooling, and the article archive.

## Content ownership and redistribution

All materials in this repository are owned by or licensed to WallStreetGuide.net and may be redistributed by the team. Nothing is excluded from version control on redistribution grounds; the deep-dive PDFs and keyword spreadsheets are committed alongside everything else.

## Article provenance: human vs AI

The article archive is separated by how the writing was produced:

- `published/ai-written/` — finals generated in Claude sessions under the WSG skill (includes the May-July 2026 library and the August 5 and August 11-12, 2026 batches).
- `published/human-written/` — finals authored by people. Starts empty: no article in the imported archive could be verified as human-authored end to end. Add human originals here as they exist.
- `drafts/` — working markdown drafts. Everything in this folder is AI-generated by definition of the system; only the latest surviving version of each article is kept in git.
- `samples/` — a small calibration set the skill reads for voice and structure. Samples may themselves be AI-written; they are calibration references, not the article library.

## Tooling

```bash
# Compliance gate (em-dashes, filler, listicle counts, VERIFY tags, brand whitelist)
python3 scripts/check_article.py drafts/draft-{slug}-v1.md

# Prose-level style gate (bold skim test, listicle item structure, say/dont blocks)
python3 scripts/check_style.py drafts/draft-{slug}-v1.md

# Link verifier (known bot-block false negatives: mckinsey.com, morganstanley.com,
# goldmansachs.com, BofA, Wells Fargo, Newmark, Baird, Raymond James, Lincoln,
# Management Consulted, Health Advances -- re-verify those by hand before removing links)
python3 scripts/check_links.py drafts/draft-{slug}-v1.md

# docx builder, current standard (Title / Heading 2 / Normal styles, Aug 2026 formatting spec;
# Arial, Title 24pt, Heading 2 18pt, body 11pt, black, 2.0 spacing, Letter with 1-inch margins)
python3 scripts/markdown_to_docx_v2.py drafts/draft-{slug}-v1.md "published/ai-written/{Title}.docx"
```

`markdown_to_docx.py` (v1, direct formatting, May 2026 spec) is retained for history; new builds go through v2. To change the visual standard, edit the constants at the top of v2 (`FONT_NAME`, `BODY_PT`, `H1_PT`, `H2_PT`, `H3_PT`, `BLACK`, `LINK_BLUE`), never per article. `check_article.py` includes the August 2026 fix that stops years in headlines being misread as listicle counts. `verified-brands.txt` carries ~200 entities verified during the August 2026 batches.

## September 2026 changes

- **Word counts are guidelines, not limits.** Articles run as long as a thorough, plain-language explanation needs. `check_article.py` reports length as info and warns only when a draft runs short of the format guideline; the old upper hard caps are gone.
- **Two new passes on every draft and revision:** `plain-language.md` (technical ideas in plain words at first use, one idea per sentence) and `thorough-explanations.md` (no jumpy or over-condensed explanations, plus a mandatory Thoroughness Report after delivery).
- **`intros.md`** governs every opening: rotating hook type, Stephen intro always present, WSG never looks bad in its own stories, no fake-precise effort counts.
- **`markdown_to_docx_v2.py` rewritten to the full spec:** heading styles are black at the style level (no Word-blue underneath), Title border removed, Letter paper with 1-inch margins, links inside bold text render as links, a paragraph and a list in the same markdown block get the required empty line between them, numbered lists restart at 1, and empty headings are dropped.
- Templates, brief template, style guide, SEO checklist and Stephen's writer profile updated to match.
- **Consulting facts corrected** (from the Aug 21 corrections log, re-checked Sept 25): McKinsey Insight and Bridge to BCG are advanced-degree programs, not sophomore programs; the sophomore programs are McKinsey SSBA, BCG Growing Future Leaders and Bain BEL/CREW; "Bain Build" doesn't exist; MBB now recruits about 15 months ahead.
- **Module refresh:** `consulting.md` now builds with v2 into `published/ai-written/`; `prose-editing.md` gained rule 9 (the say-it-out-loud test) that SKILL.md already cited; `formatting.md` carries the full visual spec.
- All nine skills are packaged in `skills/`. Re-save `wsg-article.skill` and `wsg-prose-editing.skill` to your Claude account: the copies there predate these fixes.

## Quick start for a new collaborator

1. Clone. 2. Read `README.md`, then `style-guide/style-guide.md`, then `.claude-skill/SKILL.md` and its modules. 3. Open the folder in a Claude session and ask for a WSG article; the skill takes it from there. 4. Run the three checkers before anything ships.

Python dependencies: `markdown`, `openpyxl`, `pyyaml`, `python-docx`, `requests`.
